# **BrightTV Viewership Analysis**

**Overview:**
-	The CEO has requested to provide insights that would assist the Customer Value Management team to grow BrightTV’s subscription base and Recommendations to increase consumption on the days with low views. 
-	User profiles and Viewership data is used to provide the insights

**Data Quality Notes**
-	The following fields are not complete/accurate: Name, Surname, Gender, Province for 920 users, Race is 1309.
-	262 of the 920 incomplete profiles are active subscribers.
-	Channels were grouped into Content Genres to support analysis. Sports, Entertainment, Kids, Music, News.

**Insights**
-	Our Core Audience is male (87,61%) with ages between 25-34 (41,47%)
-	Sports is the main driver of consumption based on views of 41.36%.
-	Users enjoy sports live events and music. Risk that live events are seasonal
-	The Afternoon has the highest sessions with 3 734, Sport Live Events contributing 1550 sessions(42%)
-	Gauteng and Western Cape indicate a strong consumption by 55,5%.
-	Free State and Northern Cape remain under-penetrated , indicating an opportunity to grow subscribers
-	Friday and Saturday  represent the busiest days and while Monday has the lowest viewing sessions
-	Sports dropped by 501 views (196%) due to Live Events not broadcasting on Mondays. Other Genre’s also dropped on Monday compared to Saturday across the board.
   
**Recommendations**
1.	Replicate what drives consumption in Gauteng and Western Cape to provinces with lower penetration. Prioritize Free State, Northern Cape and North West.
2.	Contact the users with the 920 – 1309 blanks/nulls in the name, surname, gender ,province and race fields to complete user profile and have accurate data.
3.	Market Entertainment/License new content that will appeal directly to female viewers. E.g. Home and Cooking, Beauty, Reality TV and Dramas etc.
4.	Promote other genres to sports users to retain active viewers during seasons where there is no live events.
5.	Pilot Weekend sport highlight replay on Monday’s and for off season live event, Pilot High rating Movies or Drama series to boost consumption.

**Next Steps**
1.	Secure License to broadcast other sports live events in the future to retain users and attract new users. E.g. Rugby World Cups, FIFA World Cup, CAF Champions etc. 
2.	Create Marketing campaign activations launching and promoting female audience content.
3.	Make recommendations to management to hire a Data Steward that will be accountable for the data quality of the user profile fields. This ensures data is complete and accurate timeously to improve reporting.
